# Mamaz Kremas™

**A Taste of Haiti in Every Sip.**

Mamaz Kremas™ is a premium Haitian cream liqueur brand created to celebrate the richness of Haitian flavor, culture, tradition, family, and hospitality. This repository is the official development home for the Mamaz Kremas website and future digital ecosystem.

## Brand Vision

To become a globally recognized Haitian-inspired beverage brand that brings the authentic taste, culture, warmth, and spirit of Haiti to people around the world.

## Mission

Our mission is to craft premium Haitian kremas that honors tradition while delivering an exceptional modern experience. Through carefully selected ingredients, distinctive flavors, elegant presentation, and authentic Haitian inspiration, Mamaz Kremas seeks to create products that bring people together and transform ordinary moments into memorable celebrations.

## Primary Goal

Establish Mamaz Kremas as a trusted and recognizable premium brand known for quality, authenticity, innovation, Haitian heritage, and exceptional presentation, beginning with Original Kremas and expanding into additional flavors, experiences, and markets.

## Core Objectives

- Preserve and celebrate Haitian heritage.
- Deliver premium quality and consistent presentation.
- Establish Mamaz Original Kremas as the flagship product.
- Develop innovative and seasonal flavors.
- Build a distinctive luxury brand identity.
- Create memorable experiences around celebrations and family gatherings.
- Expand distribution through properly licensed partners.
- Build a strong digital, social, and e-commerce presence where legally permitted.
- Support Haitian culture and community.
- Build a multi-generational brand with room for future expansion.

## Design-to-HTML Standard

When implementing approved Mamaz Kremas designs, preserve the approved composition as closely as technically possible. Match section order, hero proportions, image crops, typography, cream-to-image blending, product photography, decorative details, buttons, cards, spacing, colors, navigation, and footer.

Important imagery must remain sharp on large desktop displays. Do not stretch small source images. Do not use strong blur, opacity, or gradient overlays that reduce image clarity. Where the design intentionally blends photography into cream or green backgrounds, use subtle masks and gradients without washing out the image.

The site should remain a real responsive website rather than one giant screenshot. Navigation, buttons, forms, product content, and links should use semantic HTML and remain functional. For highly artistic sections that cannot be faithfully reconstructed with normal HTML/CSS, preserve the approved artwork while keeping surrounding interactive elements functional.
