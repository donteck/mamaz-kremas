# Mamaz Kremas Brand Guide

## Brand Name
Mamaz Kremas™

## Tagline
**A Taste of Haiti in Every Sip.**

## Positioning
Premium Haitian cream liqueur rooted in Haitian heritage, family, celebration, hospitality, and modern luxury.

## Vision
To become a globally recognized Haitian-inspired beverage brand that brings the authentic taste, culture, warmth, and spirit of Haiti to people around the world.

## Mission
To craft premium Haitian kremas that honors tradition while delivering an exceptional modern experience through carefully selected ingredients, distinctive flavors, elegant presentation, and authentic Haitian inspiration.

## Goals
- Build Mamaz Original Kremas as the flagship product.
- Establish a recognizable premium Haitian beverage identity.
- Expand into additional flavors and limited editions.
- Develop wholesale, hospitality, event, and retail partnerships.
- Grow a strong digital community and e-commerce presence where legally permitted.
- Create a lasting family brand that can expand into future categories.

## Objectives
1. Preserve Haitian heritage.
2. Deliver premium quality.
3. Establish a signature product.
4. Develop innovative flavors.
5. Build a recognizable brand.
6. Create memorable experiences.
7. Expand distribution.
8. Build a strong digital presence.
9. Support Haitian culture and community.
10. Build a brand for generations.

## Core Values
Authenticity · Quality · Family · Culture · Tradition · Celebration · Creativity · Excellence · Community · Legacy

## Visual Language
- Deep forest green
- Kremas cream / warm ivory
- Light natural brown
- Restrained metallic gold
- Elegant serif and script typography
- High-end beverage photography
- Tropical ingredients such as coconut, vanilla, cinnamon, leaves, and warm botanical elements
- Haitian landscapes and cultural references used with restraint and sophistication
- Soft cream-to-image blending
- Editorial whitespace
- Subtle shadows and gold dividers

## Design Rule
The approved Mamaz Kremas homepage is the source of truth. New pages should feel like extensions of the same system rather than new visual directions.
